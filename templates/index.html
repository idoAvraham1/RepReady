Best Practices

RepReady is your live coach for difficult moments—not a cheat sheet. Use it mid-call when you need the move, not the facts.

01

Don't know what to say? That's RepReady.

Blanked on a technical detail? Competitor came up you didn't prep for? Hard objection you can't answer off the top of your head? That's when you open RepReady.

02

Type while they're still talking

The moment you recognize you're stuck, start typing—not after they finish. Describe the objection or question in your own words while they're mid-sentence. By the time they stop talking, your coached response is ready.

03

Set the product before you ask

Select the product in New Chat or via the pill in the input bar. RepReady coaches from the right docs. Use All Products when the moment is a cross-product comparison.

04

Give RepReady the moment, not a search query

Battle cards give you information. RepReady gives you the move. Instead of "What is our SLA?" try "Prospect says our SLA isn't enterprise-grade compared to Guru—what do I say?" You'll get spoken-word bullets and a Next move you can act on immediately.

05

Say the bullets. Execute the Next move.

Every answer ends with a Next move—that's the point. RepReady remembers the last 2 exchanges, so when the prospect responds, just type what they said. No need to restate the background.

06

One hard moment per question

Keep it to one objection or stuck moment at a time. You'll get a tighter coached response, faster—and you won't lose the thread of the call.
