[DEALDESK — OVERVIEW]
DealDesk is NorthStar Software's CPQ (Configure, Price, Quote) and proposal automation platform. It gives sales reps the tools to build accurate, branded proposals and quotes in minutes — not hours. DealDesk enforces pricing rules and discount guardrails automatically, so reps can never send a quote with an unauthorized discount or a misconfigured product bundle. Every quote is tracked, versioned, and linked to your CRM. When the prospect is ready, they can sign the proposal digitally without leaving the document. DealDesk eliminates the back-and-forth between reps, managers, and finance that slows down the final stage of every deal.

[DEALDESK — FEATURES: PROPOSAL BUILDER]
DealDesk proposal builder features:
- Drag-and-drop proposal builder with branded templates
- Product catalog with configurable pricing rules and bundle logic
- Discount guardrails — reps can only apply discounts within their authorized tier; approvals triggered automatically above threshold
- Multi-tier approval workflow — configurable by deal size, discount level, or product type
- Digital signature — prospects sign directly in the proposal document (no DocuSign required)
- Proposal analytics — track when a prospect opens, reads, and how much time they spend on each section
- Version history — full audit trail of every change to a quote

[DEALDESK — FEATURES: WORKFLOW & INTEGRATIONS]
DealDesk workflow and integration features:
- CRM sync — quotes automatically linked to the Salesforce or HubSpot opportunity
- Expiry dates — proposals automatically expire after a configurable period
- Side-by-side comparison tables for multi-option proposals (Good/Better/Best)
- Contract generation — pull signed proposals into a contract template automatically
- Mutual action plans — reps and prospects agree on next steps within the proposal document
- E-signature audit trail for legal compliance
- Slack and email notifications when a proposal is opened, commented on, or signed

[DEALDESK — FAQ: SIGNATURE & NEGOTIATION]
DealDesk FAQ — signature and negotiation:
Q: Does DealDesk replace DocuSign?
A: For most use cases, yes. DealDesk includes native e-signature that is legally binding in 50+ countries under eIDAS, ESIGN, and UETA. Customers who require advanced DocuSign-specific features (complex routing, wet signature workflows) can integrate DocuSign on Enterprise. For standard sales proposals and agreements, DealDesk's built-in signature covers everything needed.

Q: Can prospects negotiate or comment on a proposal?
A: Yes. Prospects can add comments to specific sections of a proposal, and reps receive a notification. The rep can respond or create a new version. Full version history is maintained. On Enterprise, mutual action plans allow both parties to agree on next steps within the document.

Q: What happens when a proposal expires?
A: The prospect receives an automated email notification 48 hours before expiry. When the proposal expires, it is locked for viewing but cannot be signed. The rep is notified and can extend the expiry or create a new version.

[DEALDESK — FAQ: TEMPLATES & TRACKING]
DealDesk FAQ — templates and tracking:
Q: Can we have different templates for different product lines or regions?
A: Yes, on Growth and Enterprise. You can create unlimited templates and assign them to specific product lines, regions, or rep roles. Templates can include regional legal disclaimers, different branding for business units, or different product catalog sections.

Q: Is the digital signature legally binding?
A: Yes. DealDesk's e-signature is compliant with eIDAS in the EU, ESIGN and UETA in the US, and legally recognized in 50+ countries. A full audit trail is maintained for every signature event including IP address, timestamp, and email verification. This is sufficient for the vast majority of commercial sales agreements.

Q: Can we track whether the prospect has read the proposal?
A: Yes. Proposal analytics on Growth and Enterprise show you when the prospect opened the proposal, how many times they viewed it, how long they spent on each section, and whether they forwarded it to another stakeholder. This intelligence helps reps time their follow-up.

[DEALDESK — OBJECTION: WORD/EXCEL TEMPLATES]
DealDesk objection — "Our reps already use a Word/Excel template for quotes."
Response: Word and Excel quotes have no tracking, no version control, no approval enforcement, and no digital signature. Reps can change numbers without authorization and there is no audit trail. DealDesk solves all of that. Setup takes a few hours and most customers see ROI within the first week from reduced approval back-and-forth alone.

[DEALDESK — OBJECTION: DOCUSIGN]
DealDesk objection — "We already have DocuSign for signatures."
Response: DocuSign handles signatures but not the proposal building, pricing rules, approval workflows, or analytics. DealDesk combines all of those in one workflow. For simple signature needs, DealDesk's built-in e-signature replaces DocuSign. For customers with complex DocuSign workflows, we offer a native integration on Enterprise.

[DEALDESK — OBJECTION: COMPLEX DEALS]
DealDesk objection — "Our deals are too complex and custom for a template tool."
Response: DealDesk is designed for exactly that scenario. The Enterprise tier supports fully custom templates, complex multi-product configurations, and approval logic that handles exceptions. Many of our customers have highly customized deal structures — the product is built to handle complexity, not hide it behind rigid templates.

[DEALDESK — OBJECTION: FINANCE APPROVALS]
DealDesk objection — "Finance needs to approve every deal — this will just slow that down."
Response: The opposite — DealDesk's approval workflows make the process faster, not slower. Instead of email chains and missed replies, approval requests are routed automatically, approvers get a Slack or email notification with the deal details, and can approve with one click. Most approvals are completed in under 30 minutes vs days for email-based processes.

[DEALDESK — COMPETITION: SALESFORCE CPQ]
DealDesk vs Salesforce CPQ:
Salesforce CPQ is extremely powerful but also extremely complex and expensive — typical implementation costs $30,000-$100,000+ and takes 3-6 months. DealDesk is set up in days, costs a fraction of the price, and covers 90% of what sales teams actually need from a CPQ tool. For teams already on Salesforce but not using Salesforce CPQ, DealDesk is a faster, more affordable path to quote automation.

[DEALDESK — COMPETITION: PANDADOC]
