[SIGNALHQ — OVERVIEW]
SignalHQ is NorthStar Software's prospect intelligence platform. It monitors thousands of data sources in real time and alerts sales reps the moment a prospect shows a buying signal — a new funding round, a key hire, a product launch, a technology change, a competitor contract expiry, or a news mention suggesting a relevant pain point. SignalHQ turns cold outreach into warm, timely conversations. Instead of guessing when to reach out, reps know exactly when a prospect is likely ready to buy — and exactly what to say when they do.

[SIGNALHQ — FEATURES: MONITORING]
SignalHQ signal monitoring features:
- Real-time monitoring of 12,000+ data sources including news, LinkedIn, SEC filings, job boards, technology databases, and press releases
- Signal types: funding rounds, executive hires and departures, technology installs and removals, competitor contract signals, company expansions, layoffs, earnings calls, regulatory filings, product launches
- AI-generated outreach suggestions — SignalHQ drafts a personalized first line based on the detected signal
- Account scoring — each prospect is scored daily based on signal strength and recency
- Territory management — assign accounts to reps and route signals automatically

[SIGNALHQ — FEATURES: INTEGRATIONS]
SignalHQ integration features:

[SIGNALHQ — FAQ: SIGNALS & DATA ACCURACY]
SignalHQ FAQ — signals and data accuracy:
Q: What counts as a "signal"?
A: A signal is any event or data point that suggests a prospect may be ready to buy or worth reaching out to. Examples include: company raises funding, a new executive joins who is a likely buyer, the company posts a job that suggests a relevant pain point, the company removes a competitor technology, the company is mentioned in news related to a challenge your product solves, or the company shows intent activity on relevant review sites.

Q: How accurate is the contact data?
A: Email accuracy averages 87% globally, with higher rates in the US (92%) and UK (90%). Phone number accuracy averages 74%. All emails are verified before delivery to reduce bounce rates. SignalHQ includes a data guarantee — if an email bounces, the credit is returned.

[SIGNALHQ — FAQ: COVERAGE & ACCOUNTS]
SignalHQ FAQ — coverage and accounts:
Q: Does SignalHQ work for accounts outside the US?
A: Yes. SignalHQ monitors global data sources and has strong coverage in the US, UK, EU, Canada, and Australia. Coverage is more limited in some markets in Southeast Asia and Latin America. Contact our team for a coverage assessment for your specific target markets.

Q: Can we add our own accounts, or does SignalHQ provide a list?
A: Both. You can import your existing account list from your CRM or via CSV. You can also use SignalHQ's account database (40M+ companies) to discover new target accounts that match your ideal customer profile.

[SIGNALHQ — FAQ: AI OUTREACH & COMPLIANCE]
SignalHQ FAQ — AI outreach and compliance:
Q: How does the AI outreach suggestion work?
A: When a signal fires, SignalHQ generates a personalized first line or short opening paragraph based on the signal context. For example, if a funding round is detected: "Congratulations on the Series B — with that level of investment, scaling your sales team efficiently is likely top of mind. We help teams like [company] do exactly that." Reps can edit or use as-is.

Q: Is the data GDPR compliant?
A: Yes. SignalHQ sources all data from publicly available sources. For EU prospects, contact data processing follows GDPR requirements including data minimization and the right to erasure. SignalHQ is SOC2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant.

[SIGNALHQ — OBJECTION: ZOOMINFO / APOLLO]
SignalHQ objection — "We already use ZoomInfo / Apollo for prospecting data."
Response: ZoomInfo and Apollo are primarily static contact databases — they give you a list of contacts and some firmographic data. SignalHQ is a real-time signal monitoring platform — it tells you which of those contacts to call today and why. Most of our customers use both: their existing data platform for lists, SignalHQ for timing and context. The two are complementary, not competing.

[SIGNALHQ — OBJECTION: MANUAL RESEARCH]
SignalHQ objection — "Our reps already research accounts manually."
Response: Manual research averages 20-30 minutes per account and reps can only check accounts they think to check. SignalHQ monitors all your accounts simultaneously, 24/7, and surfaces the signal the moment it happens. A funding round announced at 6am on a Tuesday doesn't get acted on until Monday if reps only check manually.

[SIGNALHQ — OBJECTION: ACCOUNT LIMITS]
SignalHQ objection — "500 accounts is not enough for our team."
Response: The Growth plan supports 5,000 monitored accounts, which covers most sales teams. Enterprise supports unlimited accounts. We can also discuss what the right coverage looks like — many teams find that focusing signals on their top 500 ICP accounts produces more pipeline than spreading thin across thousands of lower-fit accounts.

[SIGNALHQ — OBJECTION: SIGNAL ACCURACY]
SignalHQ objection — "How do we know the signals are accurate?"
Response: SignalHQ sources from 12,000+ verified data providers and cross-references signals across multiple sources before surfacing them. Our signal accuracy rate is 91% measured against ground truth outcomes. We also include a feedback loop — reps can mark signals as irrelevant, which improves accuracy for their specific territory over time.
