[OVERVIEW]
SalesTrain is NorthStar Software's AI-powered sales training platform. It lets sales reps practice real conversations before they happen — through simulated prospect personas, mock discovery calls, objection drills, and full deal scenario walkthroughs. The AI plays the role of a prospect with a configurable personality, industry, and buying stage. After every simulation, the rep receives a detailed performance report with specific coaching notes, missed opportunities, and a confidence score. SalesTrain turns practice into a repeatable, measurable, scalable process — not something that only happens when a manager has free time.

[FAQ]
Q: How realistic is the AI prospect?
A: The AI persona is rated 4.3/5 for realism across user surveys. It handles multi-turn conversations, remembers what was said earlier in the session, pushes back on weak answers, and adapts its tone based on how the rep handles the conversation. It is not identical to a human — experienced reps will notice it is AI — but it is realistic enough to be a meaningful practice environment.

Q: Can managers create scenarios based on real prospect situations?
A: Yes. The custom scenario builder on Growth and Enterprise lets managers define the prospect's industry, company size, role, pain points, budget range, objections, and buying stage. Managers often build scenarios based on actual lost deals to help reps prepare for similar situations.

Q: Does SalesTrain integrate with RepReady Pro?
A: Yes. When both products are connected, the AI prospect in SalesTrain can ask product-specific questions that the rep answers in real time using RepReady Pro. This simulates the actual call experience where the rep uses both tools together.

Q: Can we use SalesTrain for onboarding only, or is it also for ongoing training?
A: Both. Many customers use it for new hire onboarding and certification, and separately use spaced repetition on Growth and Enterprise for ongoing skill maintenance across the whole team.

Q: Is there a mobile app?
A: Yes, iOS and Android apps are available. Text mode is fully supported on mobile. Voice and video modes require desktop.

Q: How does scoring work?
A: After each simulation, SalesTrain scores the rep across five dimensions: discovery questioning quality, objection handling, product knowledge accuracy, pacing and talk ratio, and close attempt quality. Each dimension is scored 0-100. An overall confidence score is calculated as a weighted average.

Q: Can reps see how they compare to teammates?
A: Yes, on Growth and Enterprise. The leaderboard shows team rankings by overall score, improvement rate, and sessions completed. Managers can choose to make the leaderboard visible to reps or keep it manager-only.

[OBJECTION HANDLING]
Objection: "We already do role-plays with managers."
Response: Manager-led role-plays are valuable but hard to scale — a manager can do 2-3 role-plays per rep per week at best. SalesTrain lets reps practice as many times as they want, at any hour, without taking up manager time. Managers spend their time on high-value coaching, not running the same scenario for the tenth time that week.

Objection: "Our product is too complex for AI to simulate accurately."
Response: SalesTrain's Enterprise tier includes custom AI persona fine-tuning using your actual product documentation, historical call recordings, and common objection sets. The more context you give it, the more accurate it becomes. We have customers in highly technical industries including cybersecurity, fintech, and enterprise infrastructure.

Objection: "Reps won't engage with training tools."
Response: SalesTrain is built around short, focused sessions — 5-10 minutes for an objection drill, 20-30 for a full simulation. It also has a leaderboard and certification system that creates genuine competition and achievement. Average session completion rate across our customer base is 84%, which is significantly above industry average for training tools.

Objection: "We use an LMS already — why do we need another tool?"
Response: LMS platforms are great for static content — videos, quizzes, documents. They don't offer interactive practice. SalesTrain fills the gap between learning content and real calls. On Enterprise, SalesTrain integrates directly with major LMS platforms so it appears within your existing learning environment.

[COMPETITION]
Primary competitors: Rehearsal (formerly Ramp), Mindtickle, Allego, Second Nature AI

SalesTrain vs Mindtickle:
Mindtickle is a comprehensive sales readiness platform with strong content management features, but is priced for large enterprises and typically requires a 12-month minimum contract. SalesTrain is available from $39/user/month with no annual commitment on Starter, making it accessible for mid-market teams.

SalesTrain vs Second Nature AI:
Second Nature is a direct competitor in AI sales simulation. SalesTrain differentiates through its native integration with RepReady Pro (allowing product-grounded simulations), its spaced repetition engine, and its bundle pricing when purchased with other NorthStar products.

SalesTrain vs Allego:
Allego focuses heavily on video coaching and content sharing. SalesTrain focuses on interactive AI simulation and practice. Teams that need both video review and AI practice sometimes use both, but SalesTrain's text and voice simulation modes cover most practice use cases without requiring video recording.
