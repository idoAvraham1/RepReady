[OVERVIEW]
SalesTrain is an on-demand sales training and certification platform built for modern revenue teams. It combines structured learning paths with AI-driven role-play simulations, enabling reps to practice objection handling, discovery calls, and product demos in a safe, scored environment before they engage live buyers. SalesTrain tracks skill acquisition from hire through quota attainment and provides managers with visibility into rep readiness at every stage.

[FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS]
Q: How realistic are the AI role-play personas?
A: Personas are trained on anonymized data from 500,000+ sales calls. They adapt their objection intensity based on the rep's performance mid-session, simulating a real buyer who pushes back harder when sensing weakness.

Q: Can we build custom courses?
A: Scale and Enterprise plans include a drag-and-drop course builder. Upload slides, video, quizzes, and branching scenarios with no coding required.

Q: How does SalesTrain measure ROI?
A: SalesTrain's Quota Correlation Dashboard tracks the statistical relationship between training completion/scores and quota attainment within your org. Average customers see a 22% reduction in ramp time (source: SalesTrain 2024 Customer Benchmark Report).

Q: Does SalesTrain integrate with our existing LMS?
A: Enterprise plan supports SCIM-based user sync and SCORM/xAPI content export so courses can run within your existing LMS. Native integrations with Workramp, Lessonly, and Cornerstone are available.
