[OVERVIEW]
DealDesk is a collaborative deal-room and proposal automation platform that helps sales teams close enterprise deals faster. Reps create a branded, interactive deal room for each opportunity — a shared digital workspace where prospects can review proposals, pricing, case studies, and contract documents. DealDesk tracks prospect engagement (who viewed what and for how long), sends real-time rep alerts on buyer activity, and automates proposal generation from CRM data using AI.

[COMPETITIVE DIFFERENTIATORS]
vs. Proposify: DealDesk includes real-time buyer engagement intelligence and Mutual Action Plans, which Proposify does not. DealDesk's AI proposal generation is superior — Proposify requires manual template population.
vs. PandaDoc: DealDesk is more focused on multi-stakeholder enterprise deals with its stakeholder mapping and deal room concept. PandaDoc is stronger for high-volume, simple document workflows. DealDesk's engagement analytics are significantly deeper.
vs. Dock: DealDesk offers a more mature AI proposal generator and deeper CRM sync. Dock has a cleaner UX for simple use cases but lacks DealDesk's engagement risk scoring and contract management.
vs. Salesforce CPQ: DealDesk is not a CPQ replacement — it complements CPQ with the buyer-facing experience layer and proposal intelligence. DealDesk integrates with Salesforce CPQ to pull approved pricing and generate deal rooms automatically.

[FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS]
Q: Can prospects download documents from a deal room?
A: Yes, reps can enable or disable download on a per-document basis. All downloads are tracked and attributed to the specific stakeholder.

Q: Does DealDesk replace DocuSign?
A: DealDesk includes native e-signature for most use cases. Customers who require DocuSign for compliance reasons (regulated industries) can route signatures through DocuSign via our integration while keeping all analytics in DealDesk.

Q: How does the AI proposal generator get better over time?
A: Enterprise customers can enable the "Winning Proposals" model, which trains on your closed-won deals (anonymized) to identify the content patterns, structure, and language associated with your highest win rates.

Q: What happens to deal rooms after a deal closes?
A: Rooms can be archived, converted into customer onboarding portals, or retained for reference. Archived rooms preserve all engagement data for analysis.

Q: Is there a character limit on deal room content?
A: No. Deal rooms support unlimited content blocks. For performance, DealDesk lazy-loads content so large rooms remain fast for buyers.
