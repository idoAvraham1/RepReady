[OVERVIEW]
SignalHQ is a buyer-intent and sales intelligence platform that aggregates signals from 15+ data sources — web behavior, job postings, G2 review activity, technology stack changes, funding announcements, and executive moves — to surface accounts most likely to buy right now. SignalHQ feeds prioritized prospect lists and real-time alerts directly to sales reps and their CRM, eliminating cold prospecting guesswork and allowing teams to focus on accounts with active buying intent.

[FEATURES]
Signal Types Covered:
1. Web intent: 3rd-party behavioral data showing accounts researching solutions in your category
2. G2 review activity: accounts comparing your product category on G2/Capterra
3. Job postings: hiring for roles that signal a buying motion (e.g., "Director of Sales Enablement")
4. Funding announcements: accounts that just raised venture capital
5. Technology installs/removals: new tech stack additions or competitive removals detected
6. Executive moves: new CRO/VP Sales hired (common trigger for platform changes)
7. News mentions: product launches, partnerships, expansions
8. Social intent: LinkedIn post engagement on relevant topics
9. Conference attendance: registered attendees for industry events
10. Contract renewal signals: estimated contract end dates for competitive accounts
11. Champion tracking: contact who moved to a new company (warm introduction opportunity)
12. Website traffic spikes: unusual increases in traffic to your pricing or competitor comparison pages
13. Support ticket volume (for expansion signals on existing customers)
14. Product review responses: tracking when accounts respond to review requests
15. Regulatory changes: industry-specific regulatory triggers (e.g., HIPAA, SOC2 mandates)

AI Account Prioritization:
- SignalScore (0–100): composite score weighting recency, signal strength, and fit data (ICP match)
- Account hot streaks: consecutive signal events within a rolling 14-day window
- "Strike while hot" alerts: real-time push when an account crosses a configurable threshold
- Team-wide signal deduplication: prevents multiple reps from working the same signal simultaneously

[FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS]
Q: How fresh is the intent data?
A: Web intent data is refreshed every 24 hours on Pro and every 4 hours on Enterprise. Funding and job posting signals are ingested within minutes of publication. Executive move signals are typically captured within 48 hours.

Q: How do I avoid my team working duplicate signals?
A: SignalHQ's team deduplication engine assigns signal ownership to the CRM owner of the account. Unowned accounts can be claimed first-come-first-served or distributed by territory rules.

Q: Can SignalHQ track our existing customers for expansion signals?
A: Yes. Customer accounts can be tagged and monitored for expansion triggers: support ticket spikes, new hiring in target departments, or technology changes indicating increased usage needs.

Q: What is the minimum contract length?
A: Annual contracts are standard. Monthly contracts are available for Essentials at a 25% premium. Enterprise contracts are typically 2–3 years with custom terms.
