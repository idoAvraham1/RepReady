[OVERVIEW]
RepReady Pro is an AI-powered sales enablement platform that gives sales representatives instant, cited answers to any product, pricing, or competitive question directly within their workflow. It combines a retrieval-augmented knowledge base with generative AI to eliminate rep ramp-up time and reduce deal cycles. RepReady Pro is purpose-built for B2B SaaS sales teams of 5 to 500 reps.

[FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS]
Q: How long does it take to get started?
A: Starter and Growth customers are typically live within 48 hours using the self-serve setup wizard. Enterprise customers complete a 2-week structured onboarding.

Q: Can we bring our own documents?
A: Yes. RepReady ingests PDFs, Word docs, Google Docs, Notion pages, Confluence spaces, and plain text. Files are chunked, embedded, and indexed automatically.

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes. Starter plan includes a 7-day free trial with no credit card required. Growth and Enterprise offer a 14-day POC with a sales engineer.

Q: What happens when a rep asks something the AI doesn't know?
A: RepReady's gap detection logs the question in the admin dashboard and surfaces it to content owners for documentation. Reps see a transparent "low confidence" indicator.

Q: Can the AI be wrong?
A: RepReady only generates answers from your indexed documents and always cites its source. Hallucination risk is significantly reduced compared to general LLMs, but we recommend human review for critical compliance or legal questions.
