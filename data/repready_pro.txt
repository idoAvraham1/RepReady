[OVERVIEW]
RepReady Pro is NorthStar Software's AI-powered sales knowledge assistant. It gives sales reps instant, approved answers grounded exclusively in internal product documentation. Built on RAG architecture, every answer is cited and verifiable — no hallucinations, no off-brand claims, no outdated information. RepReady Pro ensures every rep, from day one to year five, answers prospect questions with the same accuracy and confidence as your best performer.

[FEATURES]
- AI chat assistant with real-time streaming responses
- Knowledge base built from internal product documentation

- Automatic content gap detection and flagging

[FAQ]
Q: Can RepReady answer questions about competitor products?
A: Yes, if you upload competitor battle cards or comparison documents to the knowledge base. RepReady only answers from what you give it — it will not pull competitor data from the internet.

Q: What happens when a rep asks something the knowledge base doesn't cover?
A: RepReady returns a low-confidence response and flags the question as a gap in the gaps dashboard. It will never fabricate an answer.

Q: Can we upload video or audio content to the knowledge base?
A: Not currently. Supported formats are PDF, DOCX, TXT, and Markdown. Video transcripts can be uploaded as TXT files.

Q: Is our data used to train AI models?
A: No. All data is isolated per customer. NorthStar Software does not use customer data for model training under any circumstances.

Q: Can we have multiple knowledge bases for different product lines?
A: Yes. Growth plan supports 5 knowledge bases. Enterprise supports unlimited. You can assign different reps to different knowledge bases based on their role or region.

Q: How do we keep the knowledge base up to date?
A: Admins can re-upload updated documents at any time. The system re-indexes within 15-30 minutes. Enterprise customers can set up automated sync from Confluence, Notion, or Google Drive.

Q: Does RepReady work on mobile?
A: Yes, the interface is fully mobile-responsive and works on any modern mobile browser. A native iOS and Android app is on the roadmap for Q3 2025.

Q: What languages does RepReady support?
A: English, Spanish, French, and German out of the box. Additional languages available on Enterprise tier.

[OBJECTION HANDLING]
Objection: "We already use Confluence / Notion for our knowledge base."
Response: Confluence and Notion are great for storing documents, but they surface raw pages — reps have to read and find the answer themselves. RepReady gives a direct cited answer in under 2 seconds. It's the difference between giving a rep a library and giving them a librarian.

Objection: "It's too expensive for our small team."
Response: The Starter plan is $49/month flat — that's less than $17 per user for a 3-person team. If RepReady helps a rep avoid one lost deal per month by giving them the right answer at the right time, it pays for itself immediately.

Objection: "Our reps already know the product well."
Response: Even experienced reps face edge cases — new pricing tiers, recent feature updates, compliance questions they haven't seen before. RepReady is also a safety net for your best reps, not just a crutch for new ones. And the gaps dashboard gives your enablement team visibility into what prospects are actually asking.

Objection: "We're worried about AI giving wrong answers."
Response: RepReady never answers outside its knowledge base. Every answer includes the exact source document it pulled from. If the answer isn't in your docs, it flags the question rather than guessing. It's specifically designed to eliminate hallucinations.

Objection: "We need IT approval and that takes months."
Response: RepReady is SOC2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. We have a security and compliance package ready to share with your IT team, including penetration test results and data flow diagrams. Most IT reviews complete in 1-2 weeks.

[COMPETITION]
Primary competitors: Guru, Highspot, Seismic, Notion AI, Confluence AI

RepReady Pro vs Guru:
Guru relies on manually curated knowledge cards that require human verification cycles and go stale between updates. RepReady Pro auto-grounds every answer in live approved documents with zero curation overhead. RepReady also includes built-in gap detection — Guru has no equivalent. RepReady Starter is $49/month flat vs Guru's $18/user/month which becomes expensive at team scale.

RepReady Pro vs Highspot:
Highspot is priced at $80+ per user per month and requires a 4-8 week implementation engagement. RepReady Starter covers a 3-user team for $49/month, setup takes under one business day with no professional services required. Highspot's search is keyword-based; RepReady uses semantic vector search so reps get accurate answers even when phrasing doesn't match exact document wording.

RepReady Pro vs Seismic:
Seismic targets enterprise deployments of 500+ seats with complex implementation and six-figure contracts. RepReady serves SMB and mid-market teams starting at $49/month with no professional services required. Seismic surfaces raw documents rather than synthesized answers — reps must read and interpret content themselves.

RepReady Pro vs Notion AI and Confluence AI:
Both are general-purpose knowledge bases retrofitted with AI search, not purpose-built for sales workflows. Neither offers sales-specific features like gap detection, competitor battle cards, or CRM integration. RepReady is purpose-built for the sales moment — fast, concise, cited answers.
