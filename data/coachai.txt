[COACHAI — OVERVIEW]
CoachAI is NorthStar Software's real-time call coaching platform. It listens to live sales calls and surfaces instant guidance — battle cards, objection responses, competitor mentions, and talk-track suggestions — exactly when the rep needs them, without interrupting the conversation. After every call, CoachAI delivers a full performance scorecard with coaching notes, talk-to-listen ratio, sentiment analysis, and next-step recommendations. CoachAI turns every call into a coaching opportunity, not just the ones a manager happens to sit in on.

[COACHAI — FEATURES: LIVE COACHING]
CoachAI live coaching features:
- Real-time call listening via browser extension or softphone integration
- Live battle card surfacing when competitor names are detected
- Objection detection and instant response suggestions
- Talk-to-listen ratio tracking in real time
- Keyword and topic alerts (pricing, contract, churn risk, competitor mentions)
- Whisper mode — manager can coach rep live without the prospect hearing

[COACHAI — FEATURES: POST-CALL & MANAGEMENT]
CoachAI post-call and management features:
- Post-call scorecard with performance breakdown
- Sentiment analysis on prospect tone throughout the call
- Automated call transcription and summary
- Manager dashboard for team-wide call performance
- Coaching comments directly on call transcript timestamps
- CRM auto-logging — pushes call summary and next steps to Salesforce or HubSpot
- Call library — searchable archive of recorded calls for training
- Playlist feature — managers curate best/worst call examples for team review

[COACHAI — FAQ: RECORDING & PRIVACY]
CoachAI FAQ — recording and privacy:
Q: Does CoachAI record calls automatically?
A: CoachAI can be configured to record automatically or to require the rep to start recording manually. Consent banners are configurable to meet local legal requirements.

Q: Can prospects tell they are being coached?
A: No. The coaching interface is visible only on the rep's screen. Whisper mode coaching from managers is also inaudible to prospects.

Q: How does CoachAI know when a competitor is mentioned?
A: Admins configure a list of competitor names and keywords in the portal. When CoachAI detects these in the live transcript, it surfaces the relevant battle card within 800ms.

[COACHAI — FAQ: INTEGRATIONS & DATA]
CoachAI FAQ — integrations and data:
Q: Can we use our own call recordings to train the AI?
A: Yes, on the Enterprise tier. NorthStar's AI team works with you over 2-3 weeks to fine-tune the model on your specific industry, product vocabulary, and sales methodology.

Q: Does CoachAI work with our existing phone system?
A: CoachAI supports Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, RingCentral, and Dialpad natively. Traditional landlines are not supported. Contact our team for other VoIP integrations.

Q: How long are call recordings stored?
A: 90 days on Starter, 12 months on Growth, configurable (up to unlimited) on Enterprise.

[COACHAI — FAQ: COACHING WORKFLOW]
CoachAI FAQ — coaching workflow:
Q: Can managers leave feedback on specific moments in a call?
A: Yes. Managers can click any timestamp in the transcript to leave a comment. Reps receive a notification and can review the feedback in their coaching inbox.

Q: What happens if our internet drops during a call?
A: Real-time coaching pauses, but the call continues normally. If the call is being recorded, CoachAI will attempt to recover the transcript from the audio buffer on reconnect. Post-call features remain fully functional.

[COACHAI — OBJECTION: RECORDING COMPLIANCE]

[COACHAI — OBJECTION: MICROMANAGEMENT]

Response: CoachAI is designed to support reps, not surveil them. Reps see their own scorecards first — managers only see aggregate team data by default. Most reps report feeling more confident, not more watched, because they have guidance available when they need it. Adoption rates are highest when reps are involved in the rollout conversation.

[COACHAI — OBJECTION: GONG OR CHORUS]
CoachAI objection — "We already have Gong or Chorus."
Response: Gong and Chorus are primarily post-call analytics tools — they analyze what happened after the call ends. CoachAI's differentiator is real-time guidance during the call, when it can actually change the outcome. Many customers use both: Gong for revenue intelligence, CoachAI for in-the-moment coaching.

[COACHAI — OBJECTION: EXPERIENCED TEAM]
