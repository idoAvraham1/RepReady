[OVERVIEW]
CoachAI is an AI-powered sales coaching platform that analyzes call recordings, email threads, and CRM activity to give sales reps and managers real-time coaching recommendations. CoachAI surfaces winning behaviors from top performers and automatically identifies coaching opportunities at scale without requiring managers to manually review every call.

[FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS]
Q: Does CoachAI record calls itself?
A: No. CoachAI ingests recordings from your existing conferencing tools (Zoom, Teams, etc.) via integration. You control the recording policy.

Q: How accurate is the transcription?
A: Average WER (word error rate) is under 5% for English calls in standard audio quality environments. Accuracy may vary for heavy accents or low-quality audio.

Q: Can managers customize what the AI scores?
A: Yes. Business and Enterprise customers can create custom scorecard criteria aligned to their sales methodology (MEDDIC, SPIN, Challenger, etc.).

Q: Does CoachAI store call recordings?
A: Audio files are stored for 90 days by default (configurable up to 2 years on Enterprise). Transcripts are retained per your data retention policy.
